Bard song charge phrases
========================

Sound\FX\mus\bard\songs\*.wav
  3-second opening phrases sliced from Skyrim Special Edition
  Skyrim - Sounds.bsa  sound\fx\mus\bard\{lute,flute,drum}\mus_bard*.wav
  Bethesda Softworks / Zenimax. Vanilla tavern tracks only.

meshes\weapons\bard\{lute,flute,drum}.nif
  Copied from Skyrim - Meshes0.bsa
  meshes\clutter\common\{lute01,flute01,drum01}.nif
  Bethesda Softworks / Zenimax.

Sound\FX\mus\bard\tavern\{Lute,Flute,Drum}\*\*.xwm
  Tavern instrumentals from Skyrim's Got Talent - Improve As a Bard
  (Nexus 50357, 1.76). Rank folders Beginner / Half-Decent / TrueBard|BardLevel / Pro.
  Clueless / duo / voice files were not copied. Do not install SGT.esp.

Sound\FX\mus\bard\tavern\BA\{Lute,Flute,Drum}\*.xwm
  Extra player tavern clips from BA Bard Songs (Nexus 47202, 1.3).
  Split by instrument slot into Adept / Expert / Master pools. Do not install BA Bard Songs.esp.

Sound\FX\mus\bard\tavern\SGT\Duos\{FluteLute,FluteDrum,LuteDrum}\*.xwm
  Mixed SGT duet stems. Both nearby bards play the same mix. Do not install SGT.esp.

Sound\FX\mus\bard\tones\lute-*.wav
  Eight 3-second sections, in order, of Bard Magic Overhaul
  mus_nmbardlute_01. A short join only, so a repeat does not tick.
  Do not install BardMagicOverhaul.esp.

Sound\FX\mus\bard\tones\flute-*.wav
  Eight 3-second sections, in order, of vanilla mus_bardflute_01_sarba
  (Skyrim - Sounds.bsa). Short loop join only.

Sound\FX\mus\bard\tones\drum-*.wav
  Eight 3-second sections, in order, of vanilla mus_barddrum_01.
  Short loop join only.

meshes\actors\character\animations\OpenAnimationReplacer\Bard\{Lute,Flute,Drum}
  OAR pack gated on Core.esp staff WEAPs 21ACE2 / 21ACE3 / 21ACE4.
  Lute idle/charge/release from DwemerCore DCGuitar (Nexus 158409).
  Flute loop NpcFluteAnim, release PanFluteShortAnim; drum HandpanAnim
  from Dragon Bard Ultimate (Nexus 151231). Walk, run, and sprint keep
  that performance on the arms and the staff step on the legs. Do not install
  DwemerCore.esp, DragonBardUltimate.esp, SGT, or Free Movement.esp.

Do not copy these files into Steam Skyrim Data. Launcher patch only.
