# Patch notes

## Changes
- Improved a crowded fight. The world keeps up better while people are swinging.
- Updated severals kill perks to unlock when combo'd with other skills (mostly combat atm, will do crafting/gathering next)
- Reworked perks for armor, shields, and movement in a fight. A full set rates higher, a light shield is easier to hold, a heavy bash staggers harder, and hiding, power attacks, and dodges cost less breath.
- Fixed the world freezing on a busy night, including while the player list stays open.
- Fixed animals sinking through the street. A rhino stays on the ground while it walks.
- Added favorited weapons and shields on other people. A sword sits on the hip, a bow or shield on the back, and drawing that weapon hides the copy.
- Reworked weapon perks some more. Each weapon has a perk at every tier more rebalancing
- Added resting in a bed while logged out. Energy refills faster, and logging back in puts you in the bed and gets you up.
- Fixed knocking. A keep, barracks, temple, embassy, or smithy door can be knocked. A cave or fort still cannot.
- Reworked the wilderness. Plains are sabre cats, forests are bears, snow is snow bears and ice trolls, the peaks are frost trolls, and giant camps stay giants.
- Fixed chairs and beds. Someone already sitting stays on the furniture when you walk in.
- Fixed beds. You lie down, and time does not pass.
- Fixed spectating. Your body stays a step behind while you watch someone.
- Fixed searching a body. It no longer throws you out.
- Fixed closing your inventory or spell list. Armor stays on, and a click still takes a piece off.
- Fixed character titles. They stay on people and no longer rename chests.
- Fixed the sawmill. Taking the firewood no longer asks for Herbalism.
- Fixed water breathing. The spell and enchanted gear keep you from drowning.
- Improved the Game Master character sheet. It shows armor, resists, and active buffs, and long lists grow as you scroll.
- Fixed spectating and appearing. The camera sits on who you watch without bumping them, and a person or a mounted pair fades in.
