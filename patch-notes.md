# Patch notes

## Changes
- Updated creatures and people so higher-level ones are tougher, and a dungeon boss is at least twice as strong as the room.
- Fixed stamina and poise sticking or jumping when you sit, heal, or empty the bar. A perfect parry fills poise, and tapping block no longer keeps landing parries.
- Added taking a helmet off into your hand and onto your hip, a coin pouch that grows with the gold you carry, and a short wait when you put armor on or take it off.
- Added prompts while you are down: call for help, and release to the temple when your time is up.
- Fixed swinging a bow or crossbow at someone. That no longer gives experience. Firing still does.
- Added an admin menu on F7. Your rank decides which tools you see.
