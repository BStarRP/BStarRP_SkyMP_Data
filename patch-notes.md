# Patch notes

## Changes
- Added a movement speed control for staff. The admin menu, /speed, and the mouse wheel while noclip all set it.
- Fixed putting a helmet back on. It uses the one parked at your hip.
- Improved the load screen. The level bar shows how far you are through the current level.
- Fixed sneak attacks. Backstab, Deadly Aim, and Assassin's Blade stack, so a dagger needs both Backstab and Assassin's Blade for the full bonus.
- Fixed knocking. People outside and people inside both hear it at the door.
- Fixed calm, fear, frenzy, and turn dropping when your group is fighting nearby.
- Improved swimming. Each piece of heavy armor slows you in the water.
- Updated flesh spells. They last about five minutes, and Stability makes them last longer.
- Improved training. Someone further ahead in the skill they have earned teaches a little more, and both of you are told.
- Fixed taking from a house you do not own. Without a key, that is theft.
- Updated mining. Quicksilver ore takes more skill than moonstone.
- Improved armor. It resists slashing, piercing, and blunt blows, and each type still favors some of those more than others.
- Improved Interest. Changing weapons makes things more interesting.
- Fixed powers and racial abilities lasting longer than they should.
- Fixed Knifepoint Ridge staying closed.
- Fixed the camera jumping when you change areas.
- Fixed crafting stations crashing when you open them.
- Fixed healing. A light heal closes a bruise, a mid heal closes a cut, and a master heal closes a deep cut. Your health still comes back while the wound is open.
- Added a Reports page in the admin menu. It shows who sent the report and their screenshot, and marks that player REPORT until you clear it.
- Added a way to clear a player's anti-cheat flag from the admin menu.
- Fixed looking around while you are cuffed, shackled, escorted, or carried.
