# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed enchanting so you can enchant several items in one sitting, and a failed enchant is gone when you stand up.
- Fixed the Hall of the Vigilant so the lodge is standing, and Helgen stays rebuilt without the town catching fire.
- Fixed archers so they draw and shoot, and pickpocket now lists what someone is carrying.
- Updating target system to lock on better
- Fixed weapon enchantments so they hit the person you strike instead of you, and magic resistance reduces that magicka and stamina damage.
- Fixed going down so you drop into the wounded idle, including when a knockdown ends at the same time. Getting up no longer puts your weapon away.
- Fixed other players on horseback so they sit in the saddle, the horse walks, and they appear together instead of the rider showing up first.
- Fixed the day of the week showing Friday on Thursday.
- Fixed opening doors and using objects while on a horse. You can still dismount.
- Updated the report menu so your target shows as the title you see before an introduction, not their name.
- Fixed dispelling a spell so health, magicka, or stamina taken by an instant effect comes back.
- Fixed Vampire Lord form so it stays until you transform back, other players see you feed, and granting vampirism no longer makes heads disappear.
