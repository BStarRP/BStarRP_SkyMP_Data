# Patch notes

## Changes
- Updated spell learning so Intelligence and Willpower set how many spells you can hold, how fast a new lesson finishes, and when you can study two at once.
- Fixed Archon's Ward so it absorbs hostile spells while it is up, including Silence and Paralyze, and a successful absorb returns some magicka.
- Updated Silence so a weapon or spell hit breaks it. Poison, bleeding, and channeled beams leave it up. Scrolls and staves still cast.
- Added Drain Life, Vampiric Bolt, Blood Lance, and the other blood spells as your Vampirism rank rises, while feeding still decides Drain, Servant, and Seduction.
