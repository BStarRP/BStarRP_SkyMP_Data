# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed stray fighters that showed up and attacked you even though nobody else could see them.
- Fixed Disarm so your weapon actually leaves your hand.
- Fixed other players spinning in place after they stop moving.
- Fixed a crafted carry-weight enchant so equipping it adds the amount shown on the item.
- Fixed new enchants so the item matches the enchanting table. Older enchanted gear keeps its old strength until you enchant it again.
- Fixed executions so the weapon stays out through the wipe and the finishing swing.
- Fixed pets so you can interact with them again after taming them or taking them through a door.
- Fixed a freeze that happened around animals.
- Added Vampirism and Lycanthropy experience from using those powers, including claw attacks while transformed.
- Fixed other players' spells and staves so you see the cast, and a staff shot comes from the staff.
- Fixed calling powers so they stay on your spell list, go grey when you use them, and come back after you rest. Mass Mesmerize glows green on everyone it holds.
- Added a drunk stumble after drinking too much alcohol. It wears off on its own.
- Improved crowded fights so the world stays smoother when lots of people are swinging.
- Fixed other players' bow shots so they play when their weapon is out.
- Added a tier and skill on weapons and armor in your inventory, and matched the inventory and settings colors to the skills menu.
