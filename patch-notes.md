# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed a hitch and a quest popup from quests that are supposed to stay hidden.
- Updated the corner clock so your current place stays above the time, and the location popup no longer covers it.
- Fixed the Broken condition label so the B stays capital in your inventory.
- Updated the corner clock so it shows the weekday, the time of day, and a holiday under the time when there is one.
- Updated overhead chat so it floats on its own, shrinks as you walk away, shows dots while someone is typing, and uses white for out-of-character lines.
- Fixed creatures appearing in small groups, which could freeze the game while they loaded in.
- Fixed a hitch when a person or creature stands up the moment they appear.
- Improved compass marks for your group, pets, and marked targets so they sit in the middle of the bar.
- Fixed inventory and containers clicking rapidly and dropping frames while the durability column is showing.
