# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed chests and your inventory taking several seconds to open when they hold a large stack of the same item.
- Fixed other players not seeing a shield when you equip one.
- Fixed a summon staying in the world after you log off or the server restarts. A pet still comes back with you.
- Added knocking on a door, which tells the people inside and marks that door on their compass. Slash opens the skills menu on the stats page, and Tab opens the skills page.
- Fixed copies of the same item keeping their own durability and name when you store, equip, or drop them.
