# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed vampire Detect All so nearby people and creatures show up when you cast it.
- Fixed a crash that could take the server down while someone was riding a horse.
- Fixed attacks while drawing or sheathing a weapon, and One-Handed experience when your other hand holds a spell.
- Fixed enchanting so a failed attempt leaves the item plain, and the weapon's damage matches the enchanting menu.
- Updated temperature so you start freezing below 30 degrees, clothes and armor keep you warmer, swimming cools you, and fire, frost, freeze, and slow change how warm you feel while they last.
- Fixed the Hall of the Vigilant so the lodge is standing, and Helgen stays rebuilt without the town catching fire.
- Fixed food and drink that raise or lower magicka and stamina so the bar matches the console.
- Fixed powers so they stay on your spell list after their rest, and stay equipped or favorited if you already had them that way.
- Improved other players so they no longer jerk when they start or stop walking.
- Fixed lunar weapons so they only absorb health at night, and /heal fills your health, magicka, and stamina.
- Fixed locked doors so you cannot walk through until they are unlocked, and Lock or Unlock works with access or the right key. Forge lock kits and blank keys for weaponsmiths.
- Fixed executions so they die where they fall and wake up in the Soul Cairn on the normal respawn. The finishing swing no longer steps forward.
- Fixed chat so typing no longer moves you or uses your other keys, and Escape still closes the chat.
- Fixed a failed enchant so the item stays plain and you do not get an extra weapon with no charge.
- Fixed draugr so a hit makes them flinch instead of falling over.
- Added New Callings, Mauler for warhammers, Hewer for one-handed axes, Reaver for battleaxes, pikes, glaives, and halberds, and Arbalest for crossbows.
- Fixed flames, frostbite, sparks, and staff bolts so other players can see them, including fear and turn-undead staves.
- Fixed Fear, Calm, Rally, Turn Undead, Paralyze, and Banish so other players can see those casts.
- Removed malachite, ebony and stalhrim ores
- Removed legendary artifacts from fishing
