# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed dying from a short hop or a step off a small bank. A real fall still hurts.
- Added notice boards for the Thalmor, Legion, Penitus Oculatus, College, Vigilants, East Empire Company, Thieves Guild, and Volkihar. Each group keeps its own notices.
- Added notice boards for every lore faction (Thalmor, Imperial, Penitus Oculatus, College, Vigilants, EEC, Thieves Guild and Volkihar), placed in the world.
- Added staff meshes for the bladestaff weapons.
- Updated Solitude with red roofs, with mipmaps added to fix the roof textures.
- Updated Solitude with big changes in preparation for its overhaul.
- Removed Saviik's Solitude as a master, so the old overhaul can be removed.
- Reworked every fishing leveled list, so fishing no longer gives ebony or legendary items.
- Reduced the duration of the ash cloud.
- Updated the Morthal temple to Zenithar.
- Improved cleanup of a faction base.
- Improved how npc's are spawned, loaded, and fixed issues that may cause a load freeze
