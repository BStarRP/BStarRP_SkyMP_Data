# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed wild animals fighting each other. Wolves, bears, deer, spiders, and spriggans leave each other and horses alone, and bandits leave horses alone.
- Fixed opening your inventory or spell list. Armor stays on, and a click still takes a piece off.
- Fixed hosting a body. A corpse stops moving, and a claimed actor stays with you until that host starts.
- Fixed summon spells. They stay on the creature, not in your spell list.
- Fixed getting on a horse. It only starts from the left side.
- Fixed ghosts in Sovngarde and the Soul Cairn. They keep the summon look, and a person already in the room shows up.
- Fixed a dying horse. The rider gets down before the body drops.
- Fixed the flute. Drawing it hides the copy on the back.
- Updated the Game Master weather page. The sky and the hour are named.
- Fixed Breezehome. The furnished rooms show, and the empty ones stay hidden.
- Fixed the season when you log in. October stays autumn instead of turning to summer.
- Fixed spectating and admin invis. Other people do not see that body, and it no longer bumps them.
- Fixed putting armor on and taking it off. Other people see that animation.
- Fixed the Game Master world list. Enabling or disabling an object updates that row right away.
- Fixed horses and animals on hills. They lean with the slope and walk instead of sliding.
- Added shop ledgers for wages and sales, and hold tax that stays in the stone until a treasury chest is tagged.
- Updated spell practice. Casting the spell you are learning trims a little time, and the lesson still takes days.
