# Patch notes

## Changes
- Improved distant creatures so they stay visible a little farther, and a closer player takes over when one flies away.
- Fixed weapon fear, calm, frenzy, and paralyze so a hit that does not take hold leaves the target fighting.
- Improved creature fights so they chase whoever hurt them most, walk home when the fight is over, and do not turn back at the edge unless they are hit or someone is in their face.
- Fixed enchantments that reduce armor rating. They lower the target's armor instead of raising it.
- Fixed dual-wield power attacks so each hit lands and spends its share of stamina, and a clean hit gives half of that share back.
- Fixed tempered weapons and armor keeping their condition when you leave the bench or move them through a chest.
