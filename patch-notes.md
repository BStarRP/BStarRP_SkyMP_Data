# Patch notes

## Changes
- Removed the added boss in Reachwind Eyrie. The Tower of Mzark boss stays with Blackreach.
- Fixed putting on or taking off armor, clothing, and jewelry so it finishes when the wait does, and cancelling leaves it unchanged.
- Fixed healing lights that stayed and froze people, magicka that refilled while casting, and scrolls that came back after use.
- Fixed crashes in combat, when someone nearby mounts a horse, and when entering Volkihar Keep, and stopped extra world bosses from appearing.
- Fixed the admin menu so typing stays in the menu, Appearance no longer traps you, Bring sets someone in front of you, and you can give or take items.
- Fixed trapper bonuses that stopped after an animal came back, and named keys or items that vanished from the plain stack when you gave one away.
- Fixed Luck on an animal you killed, so looting it can still turn up an extra piece.
- Updated removing a person or creature so they disappear right after they are disabled.
- Fixed a new pet that ignores you until you log back in, a vampire veil that drops health and stamina and leaves the eyes showing, and Healing Hands or Heal Other healing you, hiding the dual cast, and skipping Respite stamina.
- Fixed filling a waterskin
- Fixed Parry breaking dual wield stance, switched to middle mouse key by default
- Fixed spell beams that left the caster, and a second arrow when a shot landed. 
- Added Settings button to reset keys to default + moved all the keys  
- Updated prompt while you are down shows the time you have left.
- Fixed milk so drinking it only quenches thirst, and lit the Squall Gate Inn hearth while removing the skooma behind the counter.
- Fixed horses walking like a person.
- Fixed creature and NPC names that showed in all lowercase.
- Added Priest as a hold job, with First Aid and Restoration.
- Fixed cutting or changing a lock from where you are standing.
- Updated a spell hotkey so it can sit on an open hand.
- Updated Perfect Parry so it no longer says it restores poise.
- Fixed a long fall doing no damage, a soul gem staying full after a recharge, and cancelling a poison coat that still looked applied.
- Fixed Falmer and some bosses fighting with no armor on.
- Fixed town forge recipes that stayed hidden, Nord Hero weapons at the wrong tier, rhino hide that would not tan, and smelting an eyepatch into endless leather.
- Updated cutting a key so the suggested name is filled in and you can change it.
- Fixed typing in chat, menus, and the admin menu so other menus and buttons stay off.
