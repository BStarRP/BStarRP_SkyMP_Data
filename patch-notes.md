# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed calling powers so they grey out after you use them and come back when the rest ends.
- Fixed pets losing their interaction prompts after you tame them or take them through a door.
- Fixed poison spells so they keep dealing damage each second for the full duration.
- Fixed the chat box staying closed after you use it. Chat lines stay up for 30 seconds again.
- Fixed Windhelm staying warm just outside the city. The entrance and the streets now match, and both stay freezing.
- Fixed vampires freezing in the cold. Cold resistance and warm clothes now lessen the cold, and vampires take extra damage from fire.
- Fixed food, drinks, and potions so their effects wait until you finish eating or drinking.
- Fixed health, magicka, and stamina jumping to full after they empty. Swings no longer give stamina back while you are exhausted.
- Fixed carry weight and health enchants so they add the listed amount, and that amount shows on your stat screen.
- Added Sneak experience for sneak attacks. Better weapons grant more, and a bow grants less than a blade.
- Fixed health coming back while you are bleeding out or dead, including after you log back in.
- Fixed gaining Fishing experience by emptying a sack or barrel. A catch no longer uses up fish you already have.
- Fixed executions so the attacker draws their weapon, wipes the blade, and lands the finishing swing with it out.
- Fixed jumping during a performance. You stay in the song and can still look around.
- Fixed sprinting until you run out of stamina. You stay exhausted, and the bar comes back slower until you have recovered some.
- Added extra logging so a freeze shows which step stopped the game.
- Fixed crafted weapon enchants so the inventory shows the same strength the enchanting table did.
- Fixed stuttering when you land a hit. Weapon and armor condition in the inventory stays up to date.
- Improved crowds so they stutter less, and heads and walking ease through a hitch instead of snapping.
- Fixed custom names on enchanted items so they stay when the item wears down.
- Fixed shooting a crossbow so the game no longer freezes, and a spent bolt no longer pops back into your inventory.
- Fixed carrying several of the same weapon or armor piece so an enchanted or different-quality copy stays itself.
- Fixed Calm so a hit breaks it on people and creatures, the same as other control magic.
- Fixed recharging an empty enchanted weapon so a soul gem fills it.
- Fixed dual casting so buffs like Windwalker are stronger when you cast with both hands.
- Fixed stuttering when you swing an enchanted or poisoned weapon. Charge and remaining poison stay up to date in your inventory.
