# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed enchanted weapons and staffs so they lose charge when you hit or cast with them.
- Updated weapon fear, frenzy, silence, and paralyze so they only sometimes take hold, and a higher-level foe is less likely and stays caught for less time.
- Updated spell holds so they last the time in the book, then level and resistance shorten them. Ash Form holds up to level 15.
- Fixed resist messages so they only appear when a spell or weapon enchantment fails to take hold.
- Fixed pets and summons so they stay friendly, and you can tell them to follow, stay, or dismiss them, including a chicken and after a door.
