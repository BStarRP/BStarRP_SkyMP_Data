# Patch notes

## Changes
- Fixed Kvetchi mercenary armor so the steel and the cloth show when you wear the set.
- Fixed stepping through a door so you arrive on the far side and stay there.
- Fixed Fellglow Keep so the bodies in the hall stand and fight.
- Fixed carrying so a downed person can still get back up.
- Fixed the smelter so a weapon or a piece of armor melts back into the ingot or leather it was made from, a little less than it cost, and only while that piece is in your bag. Rings still come apart as jewelry.
- Added Rogue Nord armor at the leatherworking station, on the same craft as scaled armor.
- Added Nord Tribal clothing at the leatherworking station, on the same craft as Skaal clothes.
- Added Nordic Leather armor. The chest is carved at the loom from a scaled cuirass, and the rest is leatherworking.
- Fixed Khajiit armor so a Khajiit crafts their robes, masks, and plate without a writ. The craft skill those pieces already asked for is still there.
- Fixed Ancient Bear Nord armor so only the Skyforge leather rack offers it.
- Fixed your horses and animals so they stay on the ground when they follow or wait. You can still ride your own horse.
- Fixed a fight order so the animal stays on its feet and goes for the foe.
- Fixed walking through a load door so it takes you through, and you stay in the world.
- Fixed Winterhold so the keep, the great inn, and the farms stand in the town again.
- Fixed carrying the dead so a body you set down stays down.
- Improved a dice throw so the die is large and the number lands in the center of it.
- Fixed the roll line so the result follows a plain hyphen, in chat and over your head.
- Reworked the wilds so each beast keeps a homeland, and the wolves, bears, and deer still share that ground. Kagouti and guar are the plains, nix-hounds the ash, shoggoths the marsh, and kwama the mines.
